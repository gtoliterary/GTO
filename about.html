About the Consultancy

Thoughtful Strategy for Books Worth Reading.

GTO Literary Marketing is a literary marketing consultancy dedicated to helping authors, publishers, and literary professionals bridge the distance between a finished book and the readers who will value it.

Meet the Founder

A belief in the quiet power of a well-matched book.

Gideon is the Founder and Literary Marketing Consultant at GTO Literary Marketing. His interest in literary marketing comes from a simple belief: every great book deserves an opportunity to be discovered by readers who will appreciate it.

Behind every finished manuscript stands an extraordinary investment of an author’s time, creativity, dedication, and courage. Writing a book asks an author to spend months or years shaping ideas, refining language, and bringing something original into the world. Stepping forward to publish that work requires conviction.

Yet publishing a book is only one part of the journey. Once a title is released, authors confront a very different challenge: helping readers actually find it. In a global marketplace where countless titles compete for attention across retail platforms, newsletters, social feeds, and reading communities, discoverability and connecting with the right readers can be genuinely difficult, even for remarkable books.

GTO Literary Marketing exists to help bridge that gap through thoughtful strategy and practical promotional support. Rather than relying on generic checklists or noisy short-term hype, Gideon works with authors and literary professionals to clarify how a book is positioned, who its natural audience is, and which promotional channels make the most sense for its goals.

At the core of the consultancy is a straightforward principle: each book deserves a marketing approach that reflects its identity, audience, and potential. Whether a title is a debut novel, a work of narrative non-fiction, a memoir, or an established backlist release, our role is to give that work a clearer, stronger path to the readers most likely to care about it.

Our Philosophy

Five convictions that guide every engagement.

We approach book marketing as an editorial and strategic craft, grounded in respect for both the author’s work and the reader’s attention.

01

Every Book Carries Real Dedication

Every book represents an author’s time, creativity, dedication, and courage. We treat each manuscript and published title with the seriousness that effort deserves, taking the time to understand its themes, tone, and promise before shaping any promotion.

02

Publication Is the Beginning, Not the Finish Line

Publishing a book is only one part of the journey. The months before and after release determine how a book is categorized, how its page converts browsers, and how word of mouth begins to form among early readers.

03

Discoverability Requires Deliberate Care

Discoverability and connecting with the right readers can be challenging in a crowded market. Strong metadata, clear positioning, and consistent outreach help ensure a book is visible in the places where readers actively look for their next read.

04

Strategy and Practical Support Belong Together

GTO Literary Marketing exists to bridge the gap between books and readers through thoughtful strategy and practical promotional support. High-level direction is paired with actionable steps across retail pages, email, book clubs, and launch campaigns.

05

Tailored to Identity, Audience, and Potential

Each book deserves a marketing approach that reflects its identity, audience, and potential. A literary novel, a business book, and a genre series do not reach readers in the same way. We customize every plan to fit the specific work and the author’s long-term goals.

International Reach

Serving authors and publishers internationally.

Today’s readership is global, and digital retail platforms allow books to travel across borders from day one. GTO Literary Marketing works remotely with clients worldwide, supporting books at every stage of publication.

Independent & Self-Published Authors

Focused support for authors building their own readership

Whether you are preparing your first release or managing a growing backlist, we help you prioritize the marketing channels that fit your genre, schedule, and budget.

Traditionally & Hybrid-Published Authors

Supplemental visibility alongside your publisher’s efforts

We work with authors who want to strengthen their personal platform, organize reader outreach, and sustain promotional momentum beyond their initial publication window.

Independent Presses & Literary Professionals

Campaign planning and discoverability consulting

We collaborate with small presses, editors, and literary professionals seeking structured launch strategies, Amazon optimization, and reader community outreach for their lists.
