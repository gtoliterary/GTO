The Consultancy

Where books meet their readers.

GTO Literary Marketing: book promotion services for authors and publishers, worldwide.

A finished manuscript is an achievement. A published book is a milestone. Yet for most authors, the hardest stretch of the journey begins after publication, when a good book meets the quiet, crowded reality of the modern book market, where thousands of new titles appear every day and the right readers must somehow find theirs.

GTO Literary Marketing exists for that moment. We are a professional literary marketing consultancy helping authors, publishers, and literary professionals improve their books’ visibility, discoverability, reader engagement, and promotional reach, with strategies built around each book’s identity, audience, and potential. Never around a template.

Why GTO

Books don’t fail for lack of quality. They fail for lack of discovery.

The gap between a good book and its readers is where we work.

i.

Strategy before tactics

We begin with the book itself, including its genre, its audience, and its identity, before recommending a single promotional channel.

ii.

Readers, not just traffic

Visibility only matters when it reaches people who will actually read, review, and recommend your book.

iii.

Honest counsel

We would rather tell you what won’t work than sell you what you don’t need. No inflated promises, no jargon.

iv.

Long-term thinking

Marketing that compounds: launch momentum that settles into durable, lasting discoverability.
