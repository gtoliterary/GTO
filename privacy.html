Template Notice & Legal Review Guidance

This document represents a starter Privacy Policy template prepared specifically for GTO Literary Marketing. It outlines standard data handling and consultation intake practices. Prior to commercial publication and live data collection, this policy should be reviewed and customized by your legal counsel to reflect your operating jurisdiction and applicable data protection regulations (such as GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, or national consumer privacy laws).

1. Introduction & Scope

GTO Literary Marketing ("we," "our," or "us"), founded by Gideon, is an independent literary marketing consultancy providing strategic advisory, retail optimization, and author promotion services to authors, publishers, and literary professionals internationally.

We respect your creative dedication and your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our principles regarding the collection, handling, storage, and protection of information submitted to us through our website (including our consultation request form) or via direct electronic correspondence.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information only when necessary to understand your publishing goals, respond to your inquiries, and provide tailored literary marketing evaluations.

  • Information You Provide Voluntarily: When you complete our consultation request form or correspond with us directly, you may provide your full name, email address, book title, author website URL, genre focus, publication timeline, and specific marketing notes.
  • Technical & Device Information: Like most websites, our hosting servers may automatically log non-identifying technical data when you browse our pages, including your Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser type, device operating system, referring URL, and timestamps.
  • Communications Records: We maintain records of written correspondence (such as email exchanges and consultation notes) to maintain continuity during advisory engagements.

3. How Information Is Used

We use the information collected exclusively for legitimate professional consultancy purposes:

  • To evaluate your book’s existing digital footprint, discoverability, and market positioning prior to your consultation.
  • To contact you directly to schedule, conduct, and follow up on your free book marketing consultation.
  • To formulate customized promotional proposals, service scopes, and campaign timelines.
  • To maintain our business records, prevent fraudulent submissions, and ensure the operational integrity of our website.

We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information or manuscript details to third-party data brokers, marketers, or advertisers.

4. Data Protection & Security

We implement appropriate physical, technical, and administrative safeguards to protect your personal details against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure. Communications submitted through modern web standards are encrypted in transit using Secure Sockets Layer (SSL/TLS) technology.

We retain consultation inquiries and client communication records only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, complete our contractual obligations, or comply with legal and accounting requirements.

5. Third-Party Services & Infrastructure

In providing this website and managing our client workflow, we may rely on reputable third-party service providers who process data strictly under our direction:

  • Hosting & Cloud Infrastructure: Web hosting and static file delivery services (e.g., Vercel, Netlify, or equivalent providers).
  • Form Processing & Email Delivery: Secure form-handling endpoints (e.g., Formspree, Formkeep, or transactional email services) utilized to transmit consultation requests to our desk.
  • Professional Communication Tools: Standard encrypted email and calendar scheduling platforms used to coordinate advisory calls.

Our website may contain links to external literary platforms (such as Amazon, BookBub, or Goodreads). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or policies of third-party platforms.

6. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

This website is intentionally built with clean, lightweight HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. We do not use intrusive tracking cookies, behavioral ad-targeting beacons, or third-party pixel surveillance.

If basic privacy-focused web analytics are enabled in the future to gauge aggregate traffic (e.g., page views and geographic volume), they will be utilized solely to improve user experience without identifying individual authors.

7. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location and applicable privacy legislation (such as the European General Data Protection Regulation or California privacy laws), you may have specific rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: The right to request confirmation of whether we hold your personal data and to receive a copy of that information.
  • Correction: The right to request correction of inaccurate, outdated, or incomplete details.
  • Deletion: The right to request that we delete your consultation submissions and contact records, subject to legal retention obligations.
  • Withdrawal of Consent: The right to withdraw consent for direct communication at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our consultation desk using the contact information below.
