04

No Guarantee of Sales, Best-Seller Status, or Platform Rankings

Crucial Disclaimer Regarding Commercial Outcomes

Book publishing success depends on numerous subjective and external factors outside any marketing agency's direct control—including reader tastes, book cover appeal, pricing elasticity, editorial quality, market timing, seasonal competition, and third-party algorithmic shifts. GTO Literary Marketing does not make misleading or fraudulent sales promises.

While GTO Literary Marketing employs industry-leading methodologies, proven data analysis, and bespoke positioning strategies, we explicitly do not guarantee:

  • Specific unit sales volumes, revenue benchmarks, or financial return on investment (ROI).
  • Inclusion on curated bestseller lists (such as The New York Times, USA Today, or Amazon #1 Best Seller Banners).
  • Guaranteed acceptance for editorial selection programs (such as BookBub Featured Deals, Amazon First Reads, or traditional media features), which remain entirely at the discretion of those third-party editorial boards.
  • Permanent search ranking positions on retail marketplaces or search engines.

Our obligation is to provide rigorous, professional, honest, and ethical advisory, strategic development, and promotional execution in accordance with industry best practices.

Client Responsibilities & Materials Submission

The efficacy and timeliness of any literary campaign rely heavily on client collaboration. By contracting our services, the Client agrees to:

  • Timely Asset Delivery: Supply high-resolution book cover files (JPG/PNG/PSD), final proofread manuscript files (EPUB/PDF), author headshots, retail links, and biographical details by the deadlines specified in the campaign timeline.
  • Content Legality & Accuracy: Warrant that all materials submitted for marketing do not infringe on any third-party copyrights, trademarks, privacy rights, and do not contain defamatory or unlawful content.
  • Prompt Approvals: Review and provide timely feedback or approvals for advertising copy, pitch decks, graphics, and email campaigns within three (3) business days to avoid launch schedule delays.
  • Account Access & Permissions: When necessary for advertising execution (e.g., Amazon Ads, Meta Business Manager, BookBub Partner Dashboard), supply appropriate guest or collaborator permissions in a secure manner.
